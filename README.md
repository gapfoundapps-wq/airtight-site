## What Airtight does

Airtight keeps the crystalline silica records a SafeWork inspector asks for. It assembles a silica risk control plan for each task and material, logs air monitoring results against the exposure limit in force on the day the sample was taken, tracks each worker's health monitoring schedule and training records, records what must be notified to the regulator and by when, and produces an audit pack.

### What it is not

Airtight is a record-keeping and compliance-tracking tool. It is **not** work health and safety advice, legal advice, or medical advice.

**Health monitoring must be carried out by or under the supervision of a registered medical practitioner with experience in health monitoring.** Airtight tracks when monitoring is due and whether a report exists. It does not perform, interpret, or advise on health monitoring, and it does not store clinical content.

Notifications to a regulator are made by you, through the regulator's own portal. Airtight tells you what is due and records what you submitted. It never submits on your behalf.

## Which states are covered

**New South Wales.** Rules are cited to the *Work Health and Safety Regulation 2025* (NSW) and SafeWork NSW guidance.

Other states and territories are not covered yet. Where the app shows a rule for another jurisdiction it comes from the model WHS Regulations and is clearly labelled as not jurisdiction-specific. Victoria in particular has its own OHS regime — do not treat the NSW rules as Victorian law.

## How the legal values stay current

Every legal value carries the date it was last checked against the primary source, and a link to that source. A scheduled job re-reads Safe Work Australia's workplace exposure limits register and the WHS Regulations every week, and again on 1 December 2026 when the new exposure limits commence.

When a value changes, the change is flagged for a person to confirm before it reaches you. Until they do, you keep seeing the last verified value, clearly marked as pending review. Airtight will not silently rewrite the exposure limit your business is judged against.

## Common questions

**Why does the app say a record is missing when I have it on paper?**
Airtight reports on what has been entered into it. Add the record and the check will pass.

**Why is the health monitoring interval 12 months?**
The Regulations do not fix an interval. SafeWork NSW and icare state that 12-monthly screening is best practice, and Airtight schedules on that basis. The app labels this as guidance, not law.

**What changes on 1 December 2026?**
The workplace exposure limits list replaces the workplace exposure standards list. For respirable crystalline silica the published value is unchanged at 0.05 mg/m³ (8-hour TWA), but WHS ministers have asked for further impact analysis on that figure, so it may change before commencement. Airtight watches for it.
